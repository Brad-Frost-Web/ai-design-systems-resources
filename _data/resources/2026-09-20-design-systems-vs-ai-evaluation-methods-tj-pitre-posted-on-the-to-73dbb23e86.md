slackSummary: "Design systems are a collection of decisions. Some are
  mechanical. Some require judgment. Some haven't been made yet. AI agents are
  now generating against these systems, and most eval conversations treat all
  three like the same problem. They're not. Put five designers in a room and ask
  whether the hero needs more breathing room, or whether the CTA is fighting the
  logo for attention. You'll get five reasonable answers. None of them wrong.
  Put five engineers in a room and ask whether a declined transaction can leave
  behind a completed order. They'll agree. Eventually someone points at the
  system"
