The practice of deciding what goes into a model's context window, and keeping it current, so the model has exactly what it needs for the task: instructions, tools, retrieved documentation, and history. It's the industry's successor to prompt engineering. In the course's context-based design system workflow, a context engineer is also a role: the person who reviews agent-assisted work against the system's standards.
