A design system built with machine consumers treated as first-class users, not an afterthought. In practice that means shipping the whole agent-facing surface deliberately: an MCP server, agent skills, generated context files, a queryable component registry, repo instruction files, and validation that catches drift automatically. The useful contrast is with systems that have merely added AI documentation, or that have nothing at all. Worth holding lightly as a label, though — the affordances that define it are changing month to month, and a system that serves humans badly does not become good by being legible to robots.
