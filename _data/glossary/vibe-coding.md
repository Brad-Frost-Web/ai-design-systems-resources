Prompting your way to a working product without a design system or close review of the code. It's fast and genuinely liberating for exploration, and the course treats it as a legitimate mode of prototyping. The trap is that the result looks done: it's disconnected from your standards, accessibility, and components, so it can't ship as-is and quietly creates a design system nobody chose.
