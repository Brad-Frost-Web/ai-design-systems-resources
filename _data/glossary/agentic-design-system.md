A loosely used term for a design system wired up for agents to use and maintain, often with several specialized agents (planner, implementer, accessibility reviewer, QA) coordinated by an orchestrator. The term is contested: the course prefers to talk concretely about the harness, context, and checks that make a system work with agents.
