The settings, rules, tools, and validation that make AI use your design system instead of inventing UI. The course contrasts two modes: off-the-rails work is exploratory and free to ignore the system, while on-the-rails work matches the organization's standards and has a path to production. Guardrails come in two flavors: prohibitions that tell the model what not to do, and tool-gating that makes the wrong move impossible.
