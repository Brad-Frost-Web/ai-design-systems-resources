Everything wrapped around a model that turns it into a dependable agent: the tool loop, permissions, context files like AGENTS.md, skills, subagents, MCP connections, hooks, and the checks that verify its work. Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor are harnesses; the model is just one part. A design system harness is that setup tuned to your system, so agents reach for your components, tokens, and rules instead of guessing. As models improve, the harness earns its keep less by propping up the model and more by encoding your organization's own decisions and judgment.
