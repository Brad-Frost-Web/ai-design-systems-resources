A durable document in the repository that says what the product is, who it's for, its goals and non-goals, and its key flows. In the course's process the spec is the first human-in-the-loop gate: people agree on the shape before any agent writes code, and every generation can refer back to it. Spec-driven development is the wider industry name for writing a spec, then a plan, then tasks, before agents implement.
