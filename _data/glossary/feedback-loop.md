The course highlights that teams often ship products and move on, so real-world usage, customer feedback, and analytics never make their way back to the start of the process. A key value of AI is making it easier to close this loop — bringing those learnings back in to improve both the products and the way the team works together. The same idea works at a much smaller scale inside AI workflows: a self-healing loop feeds the results of tests and checks straight back to the agent so it can fix its own output.
