How many tokens (in the LLM sense) a workflow consumes, which translates directly into cost, usage limits, and how much fits in a context window. Token maxing describes teams handed an unlimited AI budget who generate their way past the system's guardrails. Deterministic tools, focused context, and good design system data all cut token spend.
