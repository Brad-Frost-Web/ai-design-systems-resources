Brad narrows the lens: when this course says 'AI,' it really means large language models. Quick framing of an LLM as a large neural network trained on text/code/images/audio — a prediction machine that generates one token at a time based on the input — with a hat tip that TJ will go deeper on the AI tooling side later in the chapter. Closes on the magic trick: type words, get back the thing you asked for, a demo that would have blown minds just a few years ago.
