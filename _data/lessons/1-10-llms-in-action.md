Brad runs a wild LLM lightning tour: Wispr Flow → Claude poem ('medieval poem about atomic design') → ChatGPT image → Suno rock opera → Da Vinci video clip → Warp + Claude Code + Figma + TJ's Figma Console MCP to design and ship a working interactive website with the audio/video wired up — then duplicates everything for a space‑ballet sequel set in 3087. Lands the course's central thesis ('is it good?') and frames why design systems matter more, not less, in an LLM‑powered world: organizational standards are the bar of quality that turn LLM speed into real value, not just stuff lobbed onto the internet.
