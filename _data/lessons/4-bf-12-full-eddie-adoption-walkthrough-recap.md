Brad walks the fully Eddie-adopted bradfrost.com page by page with before-and-afters: courses, community, podcast, books, the blog timeline, speaking, music, art, about, contact, and a new More page that exposes previously orphaned sections. Four or five Eddie releases were cut along the way and the custom stylesheet is nearly empty. Demonstrates what connection to the system unlocks with a theme switcher — dark mode, TJ's Altitude theme, and a random-font-and-palette customizer — and frames the site as a primed canvas for the design language work ahead. Closes by insisting the process applies to any product and any design system, and leaves both product and system, and the relationship between their teams, stronger.
