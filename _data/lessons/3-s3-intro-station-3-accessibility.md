Brad introduces Station 3, giving accessibility its own station in the tune-up because it's so critical to a sound design system foundation. He covers the warning signs — failing Lighthouse/axe-core audits, no accessibility testing in the workflow, contrast violations, missing keyboard controls, undocumented considerations — traces them back to cultural contexts that deprioritize accessibility, and previews how LLM-driven audits and remediation (like tweaking token systems to pass WCAG) can sturdy up accessibility foundations.
