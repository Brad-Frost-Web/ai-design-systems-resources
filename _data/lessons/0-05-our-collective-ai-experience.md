Reflects on how the team got into AI through their deep design systems experience. Frames the 3 years since ChatGPT's launch as a long, messy exploration — lots of hype and dead‑ends — during which they helped clients find the AI integrations that actually work for real teams maintaining large‑scale design systems. Closes on a vivid metaphor: we're still in the Big Bang of AI, a primordial stage, but some 'clouds of gas and rock and dust' are starting to take shape into the practices that now inform how they integrate AI into DS workflows.
