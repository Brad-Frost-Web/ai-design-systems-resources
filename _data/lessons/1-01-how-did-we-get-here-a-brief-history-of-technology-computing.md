Zooms all the way out to frame the current moment: humans as a conscious, social, and advancing species that wields tools and 'general purpose technologies' (à la Mustafa Suleyman's The Coming Wave) to transform the world. Traces the arc from early thinking machines and user interfaces to networked devices, billions of screens, and now AI that thinks for itself — setting up design systems as the response to that complexity.
