Clean recap of the whole AI tool stack in one breath: the LLM is the brain (reasoning, generation — e.g. Claude Opus 4.6); agents give it the ability to take action; subagents let it parallelize and delegate; MCPs connect to your real tools and data (the USB‑port analogy); and skills teach it how to do your specific work well. TJ closes by previewing that this is the stack that powers AI‑assisted design systems work throughout the rest of the course.
