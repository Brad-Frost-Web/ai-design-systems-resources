Transparency note on the course's rollout strategy. Because AI + design systems is genuinely emerging — unlike established fundamentals — the team is shipping the course like a 'progressive JPEG': start blurry with the gist and core concepts across the whole stack, then increase fidelity over time. Two reasons: (1) get students the big picture and core demos as soon as possible; (2) leave room to slot in new and emerging best practices as they're born. The team commits to flagging which pieces are still under construction vs. finalized.
