Defines large language models as AI trained on massive amounts of text to understand and generate language, and unpacks how they actually work: trained on the open web (blogs, news, Stack Overflow, Reddit, images), learning patterns in language rather than facts — a predictive engine picking the most likely next word, encoded across billions of parameters. Surveys what they're genuinely good at: writing/summarizing/translating, Q&A, generating and reviewing code, and reasoning through multi‑step problems.
