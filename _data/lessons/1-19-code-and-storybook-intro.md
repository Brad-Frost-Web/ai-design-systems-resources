Transitions from design to code. Positions Storybook as the most widely used front‑end workshop for building and maintaining components, recipes, pages, and flows isolated from complex product environments — containing both a design tokens library and a component library. Flags the key stake: in the AI era, alignment between design and code libraries has become essential.
