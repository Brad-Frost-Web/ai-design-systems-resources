Recaps Station 5 with key considerations: traditional deterministic testing (unit, visual regression, end-to-end, integration) is still essential and AI can help establish that architecture and DevOps work; AI testing and evals are still young and a moving landscape; and humans need to validate beyond green checkboxes and give the final blessing to promote trust in the design system.
