Brad recaps Station 4: consistent language is the glue and the contract that lets both humans and AI reason about a design system, making it more maintainable and easier to use for downstream consumers and AI agents alike. He warns against bull-in-a-china-shop renaming that upends hard-earned conventions in production, pointing to version control, codemods, and good communication hygiene as the safety net.
