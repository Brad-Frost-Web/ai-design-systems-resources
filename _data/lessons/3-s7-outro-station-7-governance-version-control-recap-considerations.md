Recaps Station 7 with considerations for pointing AI at governance: a formal process tuned to your org's actual reality is table stakes, and AI can synthesize ephemera — GitHub issues, meeting notes, support communications — to paint a picture of what's really happening. It can then help create governance artifacts like workflow diagrams, better changelogs, and automated release communications, and power feedback loops that turn Slack support questions into governance improvements.
