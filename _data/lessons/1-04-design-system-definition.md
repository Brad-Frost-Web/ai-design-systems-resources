Surveys how others define design systems — Salesforce, the Interaction Design Foundation, Nielsen Norman Group, Figma — then offers Brad's own: a design system is critical UI infrastructure, 'the story of how your organization designs and builds digital interfaces.' Argues that with LLMs now handling many of the mechanical bits, the shared narrative and language of a design system matter more than ever.
