Breaks down what actually makes up a design system: UI standards, the three‑legged stool of design/code/docs assets, and — most importantly — people wielding those assets through well‑defined processes. Uses the City of Helsinki's design system as a concrete example of how all these ingredients combine to deliver reusable UI components across government digital products.
