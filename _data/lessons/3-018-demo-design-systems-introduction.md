Brad, Ian, and TJ introduce the demo design systems used throughout the course: Ian's Soul Patrol (a fictitious music nonprofit in deliberate misalignment, spanning Figma, web, and React Native), TJ's Altitude, Eventz, College Town, and CBDS systems in varying states of completeness, and Brad's Eddie — Brad Frost Web's real production, developer-only web component monorepo powering bradfrost.com, the course sites, and internal tools. Eddie deliberately has no design counterpart, and the mix is meant to show different workflows arriving at the same goal: great digital products at scale.
