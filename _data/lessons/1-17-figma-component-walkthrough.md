Hands‑on walkthrough of building a simple button component in Figma from scratch. Covers naming the file, using the text tool, 'Frame selection,' auto layout with padding, fills and strokes, creating a component, dragging instances, and adding variants (default vs. primary) so the button can be swapped in the right sidebar — showing the general gist of how component libraries work in Figma.
