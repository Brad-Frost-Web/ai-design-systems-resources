Brad pivots from the LLM demo's wow‑factor into the practical question: how does AI actually plug into digital product work? Maps the capabilities into five buckets — generate (design, code, images, prototypes, copy, data), iterate (refine, fix, ideate), translate (between environments and modalities), analyze (thinking, research, code, design, content), and plan/execute (break work into actionable steps and run them autonomously or semi‑autonomously through agents). Frames why leadership keeps yelling 'use AI' — there really is value here.
