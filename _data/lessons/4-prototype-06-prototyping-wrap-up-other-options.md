TJ wraps the prototyping showdown with where each tool fits: Lovable for building real application logic over aesthetics; Claude Design for high-fidelity designs that export to Claude Code, where you can ask for a rebuild on your design system; v0 for faithful component use plus publishing and sharing; Bolt as a Lovable alternative that actually honored the design system; and Figma Make for speed and the round trip with the Figma canvas. Notes the usual next step is pulling a prototype into your real environment to rebuild it against the actual codebase, and adds Replit and Magic Patterns as other options worth a look.
