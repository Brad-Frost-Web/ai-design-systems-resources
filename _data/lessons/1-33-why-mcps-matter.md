Makes the case for why MCPs change the game. Before MCPs, every AI integration was custom glue code through tools like Make.com or n8n. MCPs let you plug the client directly into the target platform — no dependencies, no third parties — so the AI gets real‑time access to your actual work, not stale paste‑ins or static databases. Uses a Gmail example to show live, always‑fresh context, which dramatically lowers hallucination risk.
