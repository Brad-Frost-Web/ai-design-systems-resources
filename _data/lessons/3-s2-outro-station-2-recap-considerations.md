Brad wraps up Station 2 by unpacking what "best practices" actually means: industry-wide de facto conventions, tool-specific conventions like Figma's, and your organization's own brand, UX, and front-end standards. He recommends feeding all design system assets into the AI assessment for full context, but narrowing to one asset (Figma library, code library, or docs) when making improvements so downstream users get those best practices for free.
