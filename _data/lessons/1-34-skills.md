Defines skills as reusable instruction sets that teach an AI how to do a specific, repeatable task. Skills encode best practices, templates, and domain expertise — typically a markdown file, sometimes paired with Python/JS tools. TJ's example: a 'create a proposal' skill that knows your brand, colors, format, and tone. Closing thesis: skills turn general‑purpose AI into a specialized collaborator — especially powerful for the menial, repetitive, tedious work.
