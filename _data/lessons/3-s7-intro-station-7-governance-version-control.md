Introduces Station 7 of the design system tune-up: governance and version control. Covers the warning signs — no formal (or unrealistic) governance process, missing changelogs, issue/PR templates and contribution guidelines, piles of duplicate and stale issues, and no steady release cadence — and how AI can analyze GitHub issues, PRs, contributing docs, and support channels to surface insights and strengthen governance.
