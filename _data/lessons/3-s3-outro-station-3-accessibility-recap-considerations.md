Brad closes Station 3 with the key caveats: automated checks are only part of the game (tab through experiences, run VoiceOver, do the manual verification), an accessible design system doesn't automatically produce accessible products since much happens at the composition and page level, and accessibility checks belong in the workflow and definition of done. He argues accessibility is an increasingly powerful way to pitch and position design systems in the age of vibe coding and AI-generated UI.
